# The Queer Rulebook — draft v0.1 (2026-10-06)

The interpretive layer for TimeEngine: how the classical systems get read for
queer querents, and how the systems combine into one voice.

## What this is

Classical astrology, BaZi, and tarot were written inside worlds that assumed:
everyone is straight, everyone is cis, marriage is the goal, gender is binary
and destiny. The symbols are good. The frames are not.

This rulebook keeps the symbols and rebuilds the frames — so a queer querent
gets a reading that sees them, not a reading that corrects them.

**Principle: reframe, don't erase.** Every classical rule listed here did real
symbolic work. The job is to preserve the function and remove the assumption.
Where a rule can't be reframed without breaking, it gets flagged, not fudged.

## The audit: which rules assume, and what they were doing

### BaZi

**1. Spouse stars are gendered.** Textbook: in a man's chart 财 (wealth —
正财/偏财) represents the wife; in a woman's chart 官 (officer — 正官/七杀)
represents the husband. What it was doing: naming the *partner principle* —
the element the day master must engage and merge with. Reframe: the partner
star is read from the chart's polarity structure, not the native's gender.
财 becomes the *merging* partnership principle, 官 the *committing* one, and
the reading names which dynamic is alive — or reads both, the way v0.3.0
already returns both luck directions when no gender is given. The day branch
(spouse palace, 配偶宫) keeps its function as the seat of intimate
partnership without assuming who sits there.

**2. Luck-pillar direction by gender** — already reframed in v0.3.0 (explicit
direction, classical rule when a classical gender is given, otherwise both).
This is the template for every reframe below: the classical rule stays
available; it just stops being mandatory.

**3. Children stars are gendered.** Textbook: for women 食伤 (output) represents
children; for men 官杀 represents children. What it was doing: naming the
*generative* principle — what the native births into the world. Reframe: read
it ungendered as the legacy principle. Children, work, art, movements — the
chart shows the generative channel, not what comes out of it.

**4. Day master strength, useful god (用神), ten gods** — neutral already.
Keep as-is. The ten gods never cared about the native's gender, only the day
master's.

### Western astrology

**1. Venus = the feminine, Mars = the masculine.** What it was doing: naming
two relational principles — attraction/receptivity (Venus) and
pursuit/assertion (Mars). Reframe: keep the principles, drop the genders.
Everyone has both; the chart shows their condition and their dialogue. Venus
square Mars is a negotiation between wanting and chasing inside one person,
not a war between inner masculine and feminine.

**2. Sun = father/husband, Moon = mother/wife.** What it was doing: the
luminaries as the parental axis — the inherited lines. Reframe: the Sun is the
line of *identity* (how the self individuates, what was modeled as
selfhood), the Moon the line of *care* (how the self was rooted, what was
modeled as nurture). Roles in the native's formation, not genders of parents
or partners.

**3. The 7th house = marriage (to the opposite sex).** What it was doing:
naming committed mirroring — the significant other as the one who shows you
yourself. Reframe: the house of the mirror. Partnership, not marriage; no
assumed gender on either side of it.

**4. Sign polarity called "masculine/feminine."** The tradition already owns a
neutral vocabulary: yang/yin, active/receptive. Use it. (Bonus: it rhymes with
BaZi's 阴阳, which was never about human gender either — one less translation
the synthesis has to perform.)

### Chinese zodiac

Carries the least gendered baggage of any system here. The animal is an
archetypal style; the 三合/六合 compatibilities are about elemental resonance,
not gender. Keep as-is — and note the signal: the "universal" systems needed
the least repair.

### Numerology

Essentially gender-free. Numbers never assumed. Life path, expression, soul
urge — keep untouched. The second system that needed no repair.

### Tarot (the daily layer)

**1. Court cards as gendered people** (Page/Knight/Queen/King). What they were
doing: four modes of engaging a suit — learning, acting, mastering-inward,
commanding-outward. Reframe: lead with the *mode*, let the querent assign the
face. Keep the traditional names (readers know them) but read them as
spark / surge / depth / dominion, not as men and women.

**2. The Lovers.** The imagery is heteronormative (two figures, one of each);
the function is choice and conscious union — of paths, of selves, of people.
The choice is the point, not the genders in the picture.

**3. Emperor/Empress.** Keep as principles — structure and nurture as forces in
the world, not as a man and a woman.

## The synthesis sketch: one voice, not five readings

The engine returns five systems. The reading must be one. Proposed order:

1. **Numerology life path opens.** The curriculum — the theme of the life.
   Neutral, simple, orients the querent before anything technical.
2. **Day master + Sun sign together.** The core self in two languages: BaZi's
   day master (how the self operates under pressure) and the Sun (how the self
   individuates). When their elements agree: consonance — the engine and the
   driver want the same road. When they clash: the central creative tension of
   the chart. This is the headline insight no single system gives alone.
3. **Ten gods + planetary aspects as two witnesses.** A chart with strong 七杀
   and a Saturn–Sun square is saying one thing in two languages — name it
   once, with both witnesses cited.
4. **Chinese zodiac animal** as the archetypal style — how it looks from the
   outside, the flavor over the structure.
5. **Luck pillars + transits** (timing layer, later): *when*, not *what*.
6. **Tarot** answers a different question entirely: not "who am I" but "what
   about *this*, *today*" — read against the natal backdrop, never instead
   of it.

**The synthesis rule: say it once.** If two systems point at the same pattern,
the reading names the pattern once and cites both witnesses. Five parallel
mini-readings stapled together is not synthesis — it's a report.

## Voice

The 1% register: direct, warm, no mystic fog. Name the mechanism ("your chart
does X, which tends to show up as Y"). Never pathologize; never correct the
querent toward a norm. The reading is a mirror, not a mold. Every technical
term gets a one-line plain-language gloss on first use. Pinyin always carries
tone marks — 甲 is Jiǎ, never bare "Jia" (it collides visually with 己 Jǐ).
Nothing in a reading may read like homework: present, never assign.

## Open questions (Tai's call)

- **How much does the querent choose? — DECIDED 2026-10-06, refined same day
  from Tai's feedback:** present both currents, require nothing. The first
  draft framed it as "your call," which read like homework and derailed the
  reading. The fix: both currents stay live; the reader is invited to notice
  which one they recognize, never asked to pick. Same for luck-pillar
  direction: show both timelines — the reading must stand on either, no
  selection required.
- **Practitioner review.** The BaZi spouse-star reframe is the load-bearing
  one — it should get a real practitioner's eyes before it ships. The luck
  direction reframe (v0.3.0) is the proof the method works.
- **Scope discipline.** v1 is one natal reading + daily tarot pull. Everything
  else (compatibility, timing deep-dives) waits.
